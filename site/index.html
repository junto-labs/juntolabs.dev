Work humans and agents share.

Junto is a trust-first, Kubernetes-native platform for workflows run by people, agents, or both together.

Follow on GitHub How it works

Small tools. Earned trust.

Junto Labs builds small tools that compose into one platform. Any step can be done by a person, a person with their agent, or an agent alone, and autonomy is earned from evidence rather than granted up front.

Interface first

Define what a step needs and produces before deciding who does it.

Trust you can check

Every step has one accountable owner and leaves a signed record.

Agents at the edge

Agent tools, models and protocols plug in and can be swapped out.
